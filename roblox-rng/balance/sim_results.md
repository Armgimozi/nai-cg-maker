## 6. Edge cases: can every rebirth gate be done at its stage?

A landmark whose 1-in is at or below the current luck can't be rolled, so a gate is impossible while luck is that high; the auto-discover rule does not add it to the inventory. The expected wait is for the free player at the end-of-run luck with the current AUTO speed. "Held up by landmark" is the share of simulated players whose rebirth came right after the last landmark instead of after the coins.
