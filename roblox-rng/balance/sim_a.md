## Passport-stamp blockers (landmarks never discovered by the horizon)

A roll never reaches a landmark once luck ≥ the 1-in of a rarer one (RollLogic tests rarest first and stops at the first success), so commons whose 1-in is below the player's luck become unobtainable — and a continent stamp needs *every* landmark of rank ≤ 5 in it.
